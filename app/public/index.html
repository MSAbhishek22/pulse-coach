Pulse Coach

Your webcam watches your pulse. If it stays high for a bit, the page breathes with you until it settles. Nothing here is a medical reading — it is a rough guide, for general wellness only.

Before the camera starts

  • The camera starts only when you press the button below.
  • Your video is not recorded and not stored by this app.
  • With a Presage key on the server, the SmartSpectra SDK does the measuring. The SDK connects to Presage's service to check the key and process the signal — that is how their SDK works.
  • Without a key, your browser makes its own rough estimate from the camera, on this device. Video never leaves the device in that mode.
  • Stop any time with the Stop button, or just close the tab.
